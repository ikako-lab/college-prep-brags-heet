Brag Sheet mascot options

1. Memo

Elephant

"Elephants never forget." Memo helps students remember what really happened, and holds the pencil to write it down. Calm, warm, a good listener.

2. Scout

Raccoon

A curious collector who digs up the small, shiny moments students forget they had, and keeps them in a satchel. Playful and a bit mischievous.

3. Page

Spiral notebook

The brag sheet come to life. An upbeat notebook that waves you in and takes notes as you talk. Easy to turn into an app icon.
